TurnoBus privacy.

Your logbook is stored on your device. TurnoBus requires no account and does not send its contents to a developer server.

Developer and contact

TurnoBus is developed by Adrian Morente Gabaldon. For privacy or support questions, contact support@inqredibleapp.com.

Information stored by the app

Services, dates, hours, notes, day markers, weekly goals and settings are stored in TurnoBus’s private storage on your iPhone. Imported images and PDFs are copied there. Thumbnails may be regenerated; imported files are kept as the originals received. TurnoBus has no advertising, tracking or analytics SDK. The developer does not automatically receive your entries, documents or photographs.

Camera, Photos and Files

The camera is used when you request a photograph and grant permission. Photos uses the system picker to import the images you choose, without requesting general access to your entire library. Files lets you select documents or an external backup folder. You can review permissions in iOS Settings. Downloading or synchronizing files from iCloud Drive or another provider may require a connection and is governed by that provider’s terms and privacy policy.

Backups and shared files

PDF/CSV export, complete backups and sharing are initiated by your choices. If you choose an external folder and enable automatic backups, TurnoBus writes complete copies of your records and attachments there. It has no developer-hosted backup server and does not synchronize logbooks between devices. Backups have no password or built-in encryption. iOS and the chosen provider may apply their own protections. Check that a backup is available outside the app before deleting it or changing iPhone.

Control and deletion

You can edit or delete services and attachments in TurnoBus. External backups and shared files are independent: deleting an entry does not automatically delete these copies. Manage them at their destinations. Uninstalling TurnoBus deletes its local container. A complete external backup can restore your logbook. Restoration replaces the current logbook after validation and confirmation.

When you contact support

Sending an email voluntarily shares your address and the information you include with the developer to answer your request. Send only the app version, build, iOS version and steps to reproduce the issue initially. Work documents, photographs and complete backups are not required for a first enquiry. You may ask the same contact about your correspondence or its deletion; applicable obligations and the email services used may affect retention.

Apple services

Apple distribution and diagnostic services are subject to their own settings and policies. TurnoBus does not enable its own diagnostic collection or automatically send your logbook to support.

This website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no first-party cookies, forms, analytics or external scripts. GitHub may log IP addresses for security when serving the website, under its privacy statement. Website visits are separate from your local TurnoBus logbook.

Changes

If TurnoBus’s data handling changes, this page and its update date will be revised. See Support for instructions on use and backups.

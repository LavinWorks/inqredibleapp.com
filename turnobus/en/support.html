YOUR PERSONAL BUS SERVICE LOG · IPHONE

Help for your working day.

Record your services, find any date and keep your documents with TurnoBus.

Start with the tutorial

Open Help using the question-mark button and choose the interactive tutorial. It uses a separate practice logbook. Tap Skip to skip any step, then reopen the guide whenever you need it. The app interface, tutorial and reports are available in English and Spanish. Change the app language in iOS Settings; changing language does not translate your saved records.

Record a service

In Week (Semana), tap Record service or add a service on a particular day. Enter the date, route, shift, bus identifier and notes you need. Use hours and minutes, decimal hours, or start time, end time and a break excluded from recorded hours. Tap Save to save. Only completed services with a valid duration contribute to recorded hours.

Find days and weeks

Open the calendar from Week or History. Change month or year, choose a day and open its services or week. History also lets you search routes, bus identifiers, shifts and notes and apply filters.

Add and share documents

Choose Camera, Photos or Files in the service form. Images and PDFs are supported, up to 10 attachments per service and 25 MB per file. Open an attachment from the service details to view or share it. Export data creates PDF or CSV reports for a week or date range. PDF reports do not include attachment files.

Before deleting the app or changing iPhone

Open Settings → Protect my data. Choose an external folder in Files and enable automatic backups, or export a full backup manually. Check in Files that the backup exists outside TurnoBus and is available at your chosen destination. A complete backup includes entries, photos, PDFs, day markers, weekly goals and settings. A PDF or CSV report does not replace a complete backup. TurnoBus does not synchronize logbooks between devices; cloud upload and availability depend on the provider you choose.

Restore your logbook

Uninstalling TurnoBus deletes its local data. To recover a complete external backup, open Protect my data → Restore backup. Review the confirmation carefully: restoring replaces your current logbook. Backups have no password or built-in encryption.

If something goes wrong

Keep your current logbook and make a backup before trying changes. If a file does not open, check its format, size and availability in Files. For camera issues, check the iOS permission. Avoid uninstalling the app as the first troubleshooting step.

Contact support

Email support@inqredibleapp.com and include TurnoBus, the version and build shown in Settings, your iOS version and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not send work documents, photographs, complete backups or passwords with an initial request. Developer: Adrian Morente Gabaldon.

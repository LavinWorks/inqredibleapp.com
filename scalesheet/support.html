ScaleSheet Support

Prepare model-scale artwork on real paper with ScaleSheet for iPhone and iPad.

Updated October 18, 2026

Size your artwork

Import an image or a PDF page, then use a known dimension, reference line, drawing scale or direct print size. A 2,100 mm door at 1:35 should be 60 mm high in the layout. Choose the paper size, arrange your pieces and use tiling for artwork larger than one sheet.

Print at the intended size

Run Preflight before generating a project PDF. In your printer settings choose Actual Size / 100%, rather than Fit to Page. Generate a free calibration sheet, measure its marks and configure your printer profile. Physical results depend on the printer, driver, paper and settings; the app cannot guarantee every printer's accuracy.

Free features and ScaleSheet Pro

Importing, project editing, layout, tiling, Preflight, the separate scale calculator and printer calibration sheets are free. ScaleSheet Pro is an optional one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. It unlocks project PDF generation, export, Save to Files, Share and Print, including output of the bundled sample. There is no subscription. Apple supplies the current localized price in the purchase screen.

Restore a purchase

Use Restore Purchases on the Pro purchase screen with the Apple Account that owns the purchase. Loading the product, purchasing and restoring need Apple services and an internet connection. A previously verified entitlement supports output offline; a refund or revocation can remove access to Pro. Apple manages payment and refund requests.

Projects and imports

Projects and imported files are stored locally. ScaleSheet has no developer account, developer cloud sync, advertising or analytics. Share a project PDF only through an action you choose in the system interface. Deleting the app or all local data can remove projects; avoid reinstalling while seeking recovery help.

Contact support

Email support@inqredibleapp.com with ScaleSheet in the subject, your app version and build, iOS/iPadOS version, the feature involved and the steps to reproduce the issue. Do not send private projects, images, PDF files, purchase receipts, transaction IDs, passwords or account details. We can ask for a non-sensitive example if needed.

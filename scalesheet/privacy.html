ScaleSheet Privacy Policy

Prepare model-scale artwork on real paper with ScaleSheet for iPhone and iPad.

Updated October 18, 2026

Data collection

The ScaleSheet developer does not collect, receive, sell or use personal data through the app. ScaleSheet has no developer-operated backend, user account, advertising, analytics, tracking, telemetry or third-party SDKs.

Files and projects

Imported images, PDF pages, projects, export settings and printer profiles remain on the device. Selected imports are copied into the app's private container. ScaleSheet does not upload your projects or scan unrelated documents or photos. It uses Apple's system photo and document pickers for the items you choose.

Sharing and printing

Files leave the app only when you explicitly use a system sharing, save or print action. A destination you choose may process that file under its own privacy policy and terms.

Local storage and deletion

Deleting a project removes its local record and associated assets. Delete All Local Data removes local projects, assets, thumbnails, printer profiles and nonessential preferences after confirmation. Uninstalling removes the app container, subject to the operating system's backup and retention behavior.

Purchases

Apple processes the optional one-time ScaleSheet Pro purchase. StoreKit communicates with Apple for the catalog, payment, restoration and entitlement verification. ScaleSheet does not receive payment-card data. A verified purchase entitlement is stored locally to support output offline until Apple reports a revocation. Editing, Preflight and calibration sheets remain free.

Children and changes

ScaleSheet does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children. If a future version changes these practices, this policy and the App Store disclosure will be updated before that version is distributed.

This website

These support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages under inqredibleapp.com. Website requests are separate from use of the app and are processed by the hosting provider. These ScaleSheet pages do not load advertising, analytics scripts or third-party embeds. If you email support, the mailbox receives the address and information you choose to send, which are used to answer your request.

Privacy contact

Contact support@inqredibleapp.com with ScaleSheet Privacy in the subject. Do not attach private project files or payment information.
